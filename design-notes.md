# Shermandalorian — Design Notes

Concept: Mandalorian armor modeled on General William T. Sherman's dress uniform.
Status: conceptual build work, not professional work product.

## Color & Finish
- Body armor and helmet in Union blue (deep navy).
- Trim borrows from Civil War Union officer uniforms: brass/gold, double-breasted button rows.

## Insignia Placement
- **Shoulders:** shoulder-strap boards mounted on top of the pauldrons, bearing two silver
  major-general's stars, gold edging. Stars read as rank and as armor detail.
- **Gauntlets:** flared turned-back cuff at the wrist end, echoing the Union officer's
  upturned sleeve cuff, edged with gold braid trim. Cuff flare also houses the
  wrist-plume fog emitter.
- No stars on the gauntlet cuff (moved to shoulders per 2026-10-14).

## 3D Print Guidance
- Stars, trim lines, and insignia must be modeled as **raised/embossed geometry**
  on the print, not flat or recessed — easier to paint (dry-brush, mask, edge highlight).

## Effects (conceptual)
- No real flame. Fog-machine core + red/yellow LEDs, possible low-power red laser
  through vapor. Backpack houses fluid tank, pump, battery. Wrist plume + jetpack
  vapor from one system.
